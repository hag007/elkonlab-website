# Email alerts

Get an email with the library's new papers, each with a short summary and links.

- **When:** at 05:30, after the morning scan. Daily (only papers you haven't been sent yet, or a short note that
  nothing new arrived) or weekly on the day you choose.
- **What:** the papers in the topics you choose (or all topics), with title, authors, journal, a 1–3 sentence
  summary and links to the paper and its PDF.
- **Podcast (optional):** daily or weekly, two hosts talk through the new papers in the topics you chose (2–3
  minutes per topic). The email links to the episode in Google Drive.
- **Your settings:** every email has your personal link to change topics, frequency or the podcast, or to unsubscribe.
