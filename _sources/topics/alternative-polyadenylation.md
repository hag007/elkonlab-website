# Alternative polyadenylation

**Alternative cleavage and polyadenylation (APA) and post-transcriptional regulation through the 3′UTR, in bulk data.**
- Genome-wide mapping and quantification of polyA site usage, by 3′-end sequencing or APA inference from RNA-seq.
  - [PAS-seq](https://doi.org/10.1261/rna.2581711), [QuantSeq](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.f.376), [DaPars](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms6274), [QAPA](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-018-1414-4)
- 3′UTR shortening or lengthening in cancer, proliferation and other cell states.
- The cleavage and polyadenylation machinery and the RNA-binding proteins that regulate it.
  - CPSF, CstF, NUDT21
- How APA, 3′UTRs, microRNAs and RBPs control mRNA stability, translation and localization.
  - [TargetScan](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0092-8674%2803%2901018-3)
- Genetic variants that act through these processes: apaQTLs, polyA-signal and 3′UTR variants.
  - [DaPars2 (3′aQTL mapping)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-021-00864-5)
