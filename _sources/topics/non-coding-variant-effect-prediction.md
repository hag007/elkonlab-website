# Non-coding variant effect prediction

**Predicting the effects of non-coding variants on functional profiles, such as TF binding, chromatin accessibility, histone marks, splicing or expression.**
- Sequence-to-function models.
  - [DeepSEA](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.3547), [Sei](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01102-2), [Basenji](https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.227819.117), [Enformer](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01252-x), [Borzoi](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-02053-6), [AlphaGenome](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-10014-0), [ChromBPNet](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.12.25.630221)
- Genomic language models used to score variants.
  - [Nucleotide Transformer](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02523-z), [GPN](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2311219120), [Evo](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ado9336)
- Splicing effect prediction.
  - [SpliceAI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.12.015), [Pangolin](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-022-02664-4)
- Regulatory variant scoring methods.
  - [CADD](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.2892), [deltaSVM](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3331)
