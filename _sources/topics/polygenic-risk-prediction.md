# Polygenic risk prediction

**Polygenic risk scores built from common-variant GWAS data.**
- Single-ancestry PRS methods.
  - P+T ([PLINK](https://doi.org/10.1086/519795), [PLINK2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13742-015-0047-8)), [PRSice-2](https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giz082), [LDpred2](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa1029), [PRS-CS](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-09718-5), [SBayesRC](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01704-y)
- Functionally informed priors that use annotations.
  - [PolyPred](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01036-9), [LDpred-funct](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-25171-9), [SBayesRC](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01704-y)
- Deep-learning methods.
  - [PRS-Net](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.02.13.580211)
- Multi-ancestry portability and transfer.
  - [PRS-CSx](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01054-7), [TL-PRS](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2022.09.010), [CT-SLEB](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01501-z), [PRANA](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.12.26357860)
