# Hearing loss genetics

**Genetics and genomics of hearing loss and the inner ear, limited to papers whose main subject is hearing.**
- GWAS, fine-mapping and polygenic studies of hearing traits: age-related hearing loss, noise-induced hearing loss, tinnitus.
  - [UK Biobank](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0579-z), [Million Veteran Program](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2015.09.016)
- Exome and genome sequencing and variant classification in hereditary and congenital deafness, including founder variants in specific populations.
  - [ACMG and AMP hearing-loss rules](https://doi.org/10.1002/humu.23630), [Deafness Variation Database](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2018.08.006)
- Cochlear and inner-ear genomics used to interpret hearing-loss genes and variants: cochlear cell atlases, hair-cell and supporting-cell gene regulation.
  - [gEAR portal](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01200-9)
