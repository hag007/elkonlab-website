# Integrated risk prediction

**Disease risk models that combine several kinds of data.**
- Integrating common and rare variants (main focus): polygenic scores combined with rare-variant burden or monogenic pathogenic variants.
  - [BOADICEA](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0406-9)
- Polygenic modifiers of penetrance in carriers of pathogenic variants, such as BRCA1 and BRCA2.
  - [CIMBA (Kuchenbaecker et al.)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jnci/djw302)
- Combining polygenic scores with DNA methylation or other omic scores, such as proteomic or metabolomic scores.
- Combining genomic risk with clinical risk factors, family history, lifestyle or EHR data.
  - [QRISK3](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.j2099), [Pooled Cohort Equations](https://doi.org/10.1161/01.cir.0000437741.48606.98), [BOADICEA](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0406-9)
