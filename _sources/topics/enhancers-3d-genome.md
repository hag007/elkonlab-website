# Enhancers & 3D genome

**Experimental and computational mapping of enhancers, and how 3D genome architecture links them to their target genes.**
- Chromatin accessibility and histone-mark profiling.
  - [ATAC-seq](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.2688), [ChIP-seq](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1141319), [CUT&Tag](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-09982-5)
- Chromosome conformation capture for TADs, loops and enhancer–promoter contacts.
  - [Hi-C](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1181369), [Micro-C](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2015.05.048), [HiChIP](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.3999), [PCHi-C](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3286)
- Functional enhancer assays.
  - [CRISPRi enhancer screens](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aag2445), [MPRA](https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.2137), [STARR-seq](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1232542)
- Enhancer–gene linking models.
  - [ABC](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-019-0538-0), [EpiMap](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-03145-z)
