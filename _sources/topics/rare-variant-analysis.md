# Rare variant analysis

**Rare-variant association analysis of complex traits in exome and whole-genome sequencing, mainly in biobanks such as All of Us and UK Biobank.**
- Gene- and region-based burden and variance-component tests.
  - [SKAT](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2011.05.029), [SKAT-O](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2012.06.007), [ACAT](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2019.01.002), [SAIGE-GENE+](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01178-w), [REGENIE](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-021-00870-7)
- Prioritizing coding and non-coding rare variants with functional annotations.
  - [STAAR](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-020-0676-4), [MaveDB (MAVE scores)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1845-6)
- Rare-variant meta-analysis.
  - [Meta-SAIGE](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02403-y), [RAREMETAL](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btu367)
- Time-to-event rare-variant tests.
  - [GATE](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32885-x)
- Rare-variant heritability.
  - [GREML-LDMS](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3390), [burden heritability (Weiner et al.)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05684-z)
- Large-effect risk-gene discovery from sequencing studies.
