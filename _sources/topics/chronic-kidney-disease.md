# Chronic Kidney Disease

Genetics and genomics of chronic kidney disease and kidney function traits such as eGFR, albuminuria and urinary biomarkers, limited to papers whose main subject is the kidney. Covers GWAS, fine-mapping and polygenic risk prediction, rare-variant and exome or genome sequencing studies (for example APOL1, monogenic and familial nephropathies, and diabetic and IgA nephropathy), and the use of kidney single-cell, eQTL and epigenomic data to interpret risk genes and variants.
