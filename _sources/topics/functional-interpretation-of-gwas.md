# Functional interpretation of GWAS

**Trait-level interpretation of GWAS associations: identifying the cell types, tissues, regulatory programs, genes and mechanisms through which risk variants act.**
- Aggregating predicted variant effects across GWAS loci into trait-level interpretations.
  - [IMPACT-DL](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.25.726449)
- Heritability enrichment in tissue, cell-type and functional annotations.
  - [S-LDSC](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3404), [LDSC-SEG](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-018-0081-4)
- Linking loci to genes and gene-set analysis by genomic position.
  - [MAGMA](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004219)
- Linking loci to genes by colocalization with molecular QTLs.
  - [coloc](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1004383), [eCAVIAR](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2016.10.003)
- Locus-to-gene scoring.
  - [L2G](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-021-00945-5)
- Transcriptome-wide association studies (TWAS).
  - [FUSION](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3506), [S-PrediXcan](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03621-1)
- Proteome-wide association studies.
  - [PWAS](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-020-02089-x)
