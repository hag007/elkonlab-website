# Single-cell eQTL

Expression quantitative trait loci (eQTLs) are genetic variants that change how much a gene is expressed. Mapping them in single-cell RNA-seq data, rather than in bulk tissue, shows in which cell types and cell states a variant acts, which helps explain how GWAS risk variants cause disease. This topic covers statistical methods for single-cell and context-dependent eQTL mapping, large single-cell cohorts and atlases, and their integration with GWAS through colocalization, fine-mapping and TWAS.
