# PRS

Polygenic risk scores (PRS) sum the small effects of many genetic variants, estimated in genome-wide association studies (GWAS), into a single measure of a person's inherited risk for a trait or disease. Because most GWAS have been done in people of European ancestry, PRS predict much less accurately in other populations. This topic collects methods and studies that make PRS work across ancestries: multi-ancestry and admixture-aware models, transfer learning, benchmarks of portability, and large diverse biobanks such as All of Us.
