# Fine mapping

**Identifying causal variants within LD blocks.**
- Statistical inference of causal SNPs from GWAS summary statistics.
  - [SuSiE](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssb.12388), [FINEMAP](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw018), [CARMA](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01392-0)
- Functionally informed fine-mapping, with priors that may come from non-coding variant effect prediction.
  - [PolyFun](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-020-00735-5), [PAINTOR](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1004722)
- Multi-ancestry and admixed-population fine-mapping.
  - [SuSiEx](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01870-z), [MESuSiE](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01604-7), [MsCAVIAR](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1009733)
- Detecting and handling LD-reference mismatch.
  - [SLALOM](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xgen.2022.100210), [DENTIST](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-27438-7), [CARMA](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01392-0)
