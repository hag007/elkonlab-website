# Single-cell omics & GWAS integration

**Using single-cell data to map GWAS risk variants to specific cell types, cell states and gene programs.**
- Single-cell disease-relevance scoring.
  - [scDRS](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01167-z), [SCAVENGE](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-022-01341-y)
- Cell-type and cell-state heritability enrichment.
  - [sc-linker](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01187-9), [CELLECT](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.55851)
- Single-cell eQTL and caQTL, and dynamic or context-specific QTL mapping.
  - [SAIGE-QTL](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02798-2), [CellRegMap](https://doi.org/10.15252/msb.202110663), [tensorQTL](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1836-7)
- Linking variants to genes with single-cell chromatin data.
  - [SCENT](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01682-1), [ArchR ](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-021-00790-6)
