# Single-cell omics & GWAS integration

Integrated analyses of GWAS and single-cell omics data (scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq, multiome, single-cell eQTL) to find the cell types, states and developmental stages through which genetic risk acts: cell-type heritability enrichment, single-cell disease relevance scores, cell-type-resolved colocalization and TWAS.
