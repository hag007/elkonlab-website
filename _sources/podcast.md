# Podcast

Every morning, each topic with new papers gets a 2–3 minute episode: two hosts talk through the new papers. A day's
episodes play one after the other as a playlist; use the bar under the player to move to another topic of the same
day, or to the same topic on another day. Subscribers who turn on the podcast in their
[email alerts](http://rack-shamir6.cs.tau.ac.il:8765/subscribe) also get an episode of their own topics with their email. This page keeps the
last two months of episodes; all of them are also in Google Drive (Paper Library › Podcasts).
